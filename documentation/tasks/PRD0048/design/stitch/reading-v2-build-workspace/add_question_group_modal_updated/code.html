Reading Passage 1

The Secret Life of Urban Bees

In the concrete jungles of our modern cities, a quiet revolution is taking place on rooftops and in community gardens. Urban beekeeping, once a niche hobby, has blossomed into a vital ecological movement, challenging our assumptions about where nature belongs.

Unlike their rural counterparts, urban bees often enjoy a more diverse and pesticide-free diet. The meticulously curated gardens, window boxes, and city parks provide a continuous mosaic of floral resources throughout the extended urban blooming season, which is artificially prolonged by the urban heat island effect.

Recent ecological surveys indicate that honey harvested from city hives often contains traces of up to 250 different plant species. This polyfloral diet not only makes urban honey distinctively complex in flavor but also fortifies the bees' immune systems against common pathogens.
