The History of the Bicycle

The bicycle has undergone significant transformations since its inception. Early designs were primarily novelties, often lacking the necessary mechanisms for safe and efficient travel. The evolution of the bicycle is a testament to continuous engineering refinement, driven by the desire for improved mobility and personal transport.

One of the earliest iterations, often referred to as the 'Dandy Horse', relied entirely on the rider pushing their feet against the ground. It was not until the introduction of pedals that the bicycle truly began to resemble its modern counterpart. This crucial addition allowed for continuous forward motion without the need for foot-to-ground contact, revolutionizing the riding experience.
