# IELTS Reading Task Types: Instructions and Examples

This document outlines the 16 standard IELTS Reading task types, including the specific wording used in instructions and the visual layout of the questions.

---

## 1. Sentence Completion
**Focus:** Filling in gaps with exact words from the text.

### Official Instructions
> Complete the sentences below.
>
> Choose **ONE WORD ONLY** from the passage for each answer.
>
> Write your answers in boxes 1–3 on your answer sheet.

### Question Layout Example
1. The colony of ants constructs its nest using a mixture of soil and ______ found in the nearby forest.
2. In winter, the species enters a state of dormancy known as ______.
3. The primary threat to the habitat is the expansion of local ______.

> Write the correct letter, **A–H**, in boxes 9–13 on your answer sheet.

### Question Layout Example
**Urban Heat Islands**
Cities often experience higher temperatures than rural areas due to human activity and the modification of land surfaces. This phenomenon, known as the Urban Heat Island effect, causes **9** ______ during the night. One proposed solution is the implementation of 'green roofs', which help to **10** ______ the building's temperature naturally.

|     | List of Phrases       |     |
| :-- | :-------------------- | :-- |

## 8. True / False / Not Given
**Focus:** Verifying factual information.

### Official Instructions
> Do the following statements agree with the information given in Reading Passage 1?
>
> In boxes 27–30 on your answer sheet, write
>
> *   **TRUE** if the statement agrees with the information
> *   **FALSE** if the statement contradicts the information
> *   **NOT GIVEN** if there is no information on this

### Question Layout Example
27. The total population of the island declined in the 1990s.
28. Tourism is now the primary source of income for the residents.
29. The government has refused to fund the new airport project.

---

## 9. Yes / No / Not Given
**Focus:** Verifying the writer's opinions or claims.

### Official Instructions
> Do the following statements agree with the claims of the writer in Reading Passage 2?
>
> In boxes 31–34 on your answer sheet, write
>
> *   **YES** if the statement agrees with the claims of the writer
> *   **NO** if the statement contradicts the claims of the writer
> *   **NOT GIVEN** if it is impossible to say what the writer thinks about this

### Question Layout Example
31. The author believes that learning a second language in adulthood is impossible.
32. Financial incentives are the most effective way to motivate employees.
33. The results of the 2010 study were surprisingly inaccurate.

>
> Write the correct letter, **A–F**, in boxes 14–16 on your answer sheet.

### Question Layout Example

## 14. Multiple Choice (Standard)
**Focus:** Selecting the correct answer from four options.

### Official Instructions
> Choose the correct letter, **A, B, C or D**.
>
> Write the correct letter in boxes 17–18 on your answer sheet.

### Question Layout Example
17. What does the writer suggest about the 'Mozart Effect'?
    A. It has been scientifically proven to increase IQ permanently.
    B. It is largely a myth exaggerated by the media.
    C. It only works on children under the age of five.
    D. It is more effective than learning an instrument.

---

## 15. List Selection (Multiple Choice)
**Focus:** Selecting multiple correct answers from a longer list.

### Official Instructions
> Choose **TWO** letters, **A–E**.
>
> Write the correct letters in boxes 19–20 on your answer sheet.

### Question Layout Example
Which **TWO** of the following are mentioned as benefits of the new diet?
A. Improved sleep quality
B. Significant weight loss
C. Reduced risk of diabetes
D. Increased muscle mass
E. Lower cholesterol levels
