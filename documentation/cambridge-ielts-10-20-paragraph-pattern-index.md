## Paragraph moves to teach inside any pattern

Frame/define → pose a gap → explain a mechanism → present evidence → qualify it → propose a response → synthesize or leave an open question.

The connection between paragraphs may be chronological, causal, parallel, contrastive, evidential, or a change of scale. A single passage can therefore belong to more than one family.
